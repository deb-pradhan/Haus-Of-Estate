## 1. Regulation and registrations

**1.1 Redress scheme.** Is Haus of Estate a member of The Property Ombudsman or
the Property Redress Scheme? If yes, which, and what is the membership number?

- Why: our understanding is that anyone doing estate agency work in the UK must
  belong to one of these two government-approved schemes, and letting agents in
  England must too. It is not optional, and it is a real thing in property:
  the scheme is where a customer goes if a complaint to the agent is not
  resolved. Scheme members must also have a written complaints procedure.
- The earlier assumption was that this "isn't really a thing in real estate".
  Please check with your adviser whether Haus's current activity (introducing
  buyers, listing the North London home, future lettings) counts as estate
  agency work. If it does and there is no membership yet, that needs fixing
  before more UK listings go up.
- Unblocks: the scheme name and number appear in the footer automatically once
  supplied. Until then the footer says nothing about redress.
