# Let photography lead and provenance anchor

The brighter, image-led direction the owner chose on 6 November 2026 is the right one, and the evidence says Haus of Estate should hold it with discipline rather than add features: the luxury leaders and the 2025–2026 design commentary converge on a light, warm-neutral canvas, one deep accent colour, a serif display face over a quiet sans, photography that carries the hierarchy, slow purposeful motion and very little chrome, which is exactly the Cormorant Garamond and Inter, warm-white, no-shadow, text-link direction already in the review build ([Talking Shapes](https://www.talkingshapes.co.uk/project/knight-frank-residential); [Nakada Design](https://nakadadesign.com/stories/20-best-luxury-real-estate-websites-2026); [WPResidence](https://wpresidence.net/how-to-build-luxury-real-estate-website/)). What separates a high international standard from a pretty template is not a widget but three habits the benchmarked sites practise unevenly: **facts labelled with their source and date** (Bayut labels almost every data block with its DLD or agent provenance, while Sobha, Damac, Nest Seekers and Hafren all publish pages that contradict each other on sizes, prices, counts or conversions), **open media with lightweight capture** (Emaar and Nakheel publish brochures and floor plans as open PDFs and the trend literature says luxury buyers expect "a trade, not a gate"), and **compliance rendered as trust** (Dubai's Madmoun QR code, the UK's DMCC Act price and material-information duties, redress and company disclosures in the footer) ([Bayut support](https://support.bayut.com/hc/en-us/articles/35546819623186-What-is-the-source-of-the-Dubai-Transactions-data); [Emaar, Emaar South](https://www.emaar.com/en/our-communities/emaar-south); [Placester](https://placester.com/real-estate-marketing-academy/10-real-estate-website-design-trends-in-2026); [DLD news release](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dubai-land-department-strengthens-transparency-with-ai-enabled-real-estate-advertising-governance/)). Haus of Estate's hard constraints, which forbid invented prices, availability, handover dates, payment plans and completed-facility claims and require renders to be labelled and plans uncropped, are therefore not a handicap against the market but the basis of its clearest differentiation, because UK and UAE regulators are moving the same way: omitting material information became automatically unfair under the DMCC Act from 6 April 2025, "price on application" has been treated as unlawful since May 2022, and DLD now uses AI to police advert accuracy ([Alto](https://www.altosoftware.co.uk/blog/material-information-guidance-withdrawn-legislation-alto/); [NTS](https://nationaltradingstandards.uk/news/price-on-application-in-property-listings-deemed-unlawful-as-national-trading-standards-publishes-opinion); [Gulf News, April 2025](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-property-ads-land-department-gets-ai-to-correct-listings-1.500104134)). The journey-stage patterns below translate that into concrete work: a homepage with a short search and curated entry points, a results page that obeys the Baymard and NN/g filter rules, community pages that tell the story through sourced infrastructure facts, listing pages whose facts block sits beside a gallery that never hides images, enquiry flows with four to six fields and unticked per-channel consent, a compliance footer, dated editorial, and a performance and accessibility budget set at a 390 px viewport. One limit must be stated plainly: the session's network blocked every direct fetch of the competitor sites, so all findings come from search-indexed page copies, help centres, press, app listings and primary standards documents; pixel-level observations about type, colour, motion and first-screen composition are gaps, not guesses, and "100xlab" as a quality reference could not be identified with confidence.

## The research saw architecture and copy, not pixels

Every finding in this report rests on indexed text rather than rendered pages. The proxy refused connections to bayut.com, nestseekers.com, hafrenproperties.co.uk, the seven global brokerage domains, the UAE developer and portal domains, web.archive.org and the primary standards hosts (w3.org, web.dev, ico.org.uk, legislation.gov.uk, dubailand.gov.ae), so the researchers worked from search-engine extracts of those pages, official help centres and agent portals, trade and national press, app-store listings, third-party scraper documentation that mirrors site query parameters, and the canonical URLs of long-standing standards whose content is stable but was not re-read in session. That mix is strong on information architecture, copy conventions, feature launches and legal duties, and weak on everything a designer most wants to see: heading scales, card padding, border and shadow treatments, hover and scroll motion, gallery mechanics, mobile first screens and the exact wording of enquiry forms and consent checkboxes. Where the notes could only infer, this report says so. The quality reference "100xlab" resolves only to a small Framer-centric studio at 100xlab.com describing "a team of dedicated creatives focused on web design, development, UI/UX, and product design", with a third-party profile listing it as a 2024-founded startup incubator and no real-estate work, awards or client list found; several look-alikes (100/LAB in Seattle, 100 Studio, x100lab) are different entities ([100xLab](https://100xlab.com/); [village.ai](https://village.ai/company/100xlab)). If the owner meant that studio, the reference signals a large-imagery, generous-whitespace marketing-site aesthetic rather than property expertise, and the ambiguity should be confirmed before it steers any decision. A hands-on audit at 390 px and 1440 px once the hosts are allow-listed would close most of the visual gaps in a day.

## Leaders converge on light canvas, two typefaces, and restraint

The strongest documented brand standard among the seven global networks is Sotheby's International Realty's: a single deep "Sotheby's Blue" (#002349) with grey and gold accents, Benton Sans paired with the Mercury serif, and a logo permitted only on white, blue or black ([High Falls SIR branding guidelines](https://highfallssir.com/branding-guidelines)). Knight Frank's 2020s identity refresh "keeps the existing brand colours and fonts" and its companion brochures use "a clean, minimal design and a white background that let property imagery lead" ([Red Brick Road](https://www.redbrickroad.com/our-work/knight-frank-your-partners-in-property/); [Talking Shapes](https://www.talkingshapes.co.uk/project/knight-frank-residential)). Engel & Völkers spent two years on a "digital-first" design system built around a bespoke Dalton Maag typeface and a flexible layout grid so that owned and licensed sites look the same across languages and devices ([Business Wire, 10 Mar 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230310005262/en/Engel-Vlkers-An-iconic-brand.-Refined.)). 2026 commentary reads Christie's as "gallery-calm", with restrained animation and careful image sequencing rather than heavy motion, and files Nest Seekers under global luxury sites with "understated design, where photography does most of the work", whose rule is "Skip heavy animation, let photography carry the pitch" ([Nakada Design](https://nakadadesign.com/stories/20-best-luxury-real-estate-websites-2026); [SwiftPro Studio](https://swiftprostudio.com/blog/best-luxury-real-estate-websites); [Magier](https://www.magier.com/blog/best-real-estate-website-design-examples)). The practitioner essay on the SIR overhaul captured the risk of the genre in one line: "aside from the logo in the upper left corner, it had become difficult to tell one luxury real estate website from another" ([1000watt](https://1000watt.net/blog/a-different-kind-of-real-estate-website/)).

The trend guidance that fills in the typographic detail the brand sources omit is consistent. A serif headline with a clean sans body "reads as an editorial magazine rather than a database portal", sites should stick to two or three fonts, and white space "signals the home is worth savoring, not scanning" ([WPResidence](https://wpresidence.net/how-to-build-luxury-real-estate-website/)); the suggested boutique pairing is Cormorant Garamond with a geometric sans, and the luxury-editorial pairing is Playfair Display with Inter ([Buena Vista Creative](https://buenavistacreative.com/real-estate-website-design-branding/)). Light mode "wins on readability and accessibility" while dark suits atmosphere-led sites, so the recommended pattern is a light default with deliberate dark sections ([Wildcore Studio](https://www.wildcore.studio/blog/dark-mode-light-mode)). Motion should be "used with purpose rather than decoration", with slower easing "like a slow pan rather than a bounce" ([Kontra](https://kontra.agency/top-web-design-trends-for-2026/); [WPResidence 2025–2026 guide](https://wpresidence.net/real-estate-website-design/)). The subtraction list is as useful as the additions: no busy sliders or auto-rotating carousels, no portal-style filter grid as the homepage, no more than two typefaces ([DiverseKit](https://diversekit.com/blog/luxury-real-estate-website-design)); NN/g's finding that auto-forwarding carousels annoy users and hide content points the same way ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/auto-forwarding/)). The palette direction in 2026 commentary is "quiet luxury", warm creams and off-blacks with "no primary colors anywhere" ([DMR Media](https://www.dmrmedia.org/blog/Real-Estate-Website-Design-Trends)), and whatever the palette, body text must clear WCAG's 4.5:1 ([WPResidence](https://wpresidence.net/real-estate-website-design/)).

Beneath the visual layer the leaders have also converged on a structural model: a public, image-led site paired with an authenticated client layer. Compass One gives buyers a dashboard with their search collection, tours and exclusive inventory and was used by about 370,000 clients in 2025 ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/compass-launches-compass-one-industrys-first-ever-all-one-client-dashboard-connecting)); Elliman's April 2025 "Inspirations" platform added image search, match-score recommendations, custom-drawn boundaries, shared collections and comments ([PR Newswire, 9 Apr 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/douglas-elliman-introduces-a-new-era-in-home-search-302424524.html)); My Knight Frank lets clients manage viewings, documents and purchase progress ([Property Industry Eye](https://propertyindustryeye.com/knight-frank-launches-digital-customer-portal/)). That layer is a "later" item for Haus, but it explains why saved searches and shortlists are now expected to feel advisory rather than portal-grade.

**For Haus.** The chosen direction needs no correction, only guard-rails. Keep the warm-white canvas, Cormorant Garamond display over Inter body, one accent colour and the white sticky header; reserve dark treatments for full-bleed heroes, galleries and video rather than reading surfaces. Write the subtraction list into the design tokens so it survives future contributors: no auto-rotating carousels, no third typeface, no shadowed cards, no motion that is not a fade or a slow pan, and every animation gated by the reduced-motion handling already in the build. The one thing the benchmark cannot supply is the measured heading scale and card geometry of the leaders; set Haus's own scale from the villa hero and the catalogue card, document it, and validate it against a rendered competitor audit once network access allows.

## Patterns by journey stage, with a recommendation for each

The eight stages below follow a buyer from first impression to enquiry and then step back to the trust, content and technical layers that sit under every page. Each stage states what the benchmarked sites do, what the standards require, and what Haus should take from it within the brighter direction and the hard constraints.

### Home: a short search, curated doors, and a brand-led title

Bayut's homepage is search-first, with a purpose-and-location box, a valuation tab beside it, and first-level entry points to market-intelligence tools, guides and New Projects; since 27 August 2026 a conversational "AI Search" sits alongside the classic filter search so that users "describe homes naturally instead of relying solely on filters" and can refine turn by turn, which Bayut says "complements rather than replaces its existing search filters" ([Gulf Business, 27 Aug 2026](https://gulfbusiness.com/en/2026/artificial-intelligence/bayut-launches-ai-powered-property-search-in-uae/); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/bayut-introduces-ai-powered-property-search-to-transform-the-way-consumers-find-their-next-home-469171)). Engel & Völkers' German-English homepage pairs a "Find your dream property" search with buy/rent and an "AI search" option and a "Contact your personal advisor" block ([E&V DE/EN](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/de/en)); Sotheby's offers "Properties by Lifestyle", "Properties by Destination" and "Properties with Videos or Virtual Reality" as browse doors ([sothebysrealty.com/eng](https://new.sothebysrealty.com/eng)). The pattern the researchers named "two front doors", a short structured search beside curated collections that let a browser explore without typing, is the modern standard. Two cautionary tales come from the smaller sites: Nest Seekers' homepage `<title>` is an SEO keyword string, "Nest Seekers New York Real Estate Apartment Sales & Rentals, Condos, Manhattan Real Estate", while its brand story lives on About pages ([nestseekers.com](https://www.nestseekers.com/)), and Hafren's homepage title is the single word "Hafrenproperties" ([hafrenproperties.co.uk](https://www.hafrenproperties.co.uk/)). Sotheby's rebuilt mobile-first because about **65% of its traffic was mobile** ([Realogics SIR](https://www.rsir.com/blog/take-a-tour-of-the-newly-renovated-home-at-sothebysrealty-com/)), and the trend literature warns that luxury buyers "do not want to chat with a scripted bot" even as AI search appears in most lists ([Placester](https://placester.com/real-estate-marketing-academy/10-real-estate-website-design-trends-in-2026)).

**For Haus.** The compact keyword search on the homepage with the full filters in the catalogue is the right split; the image-led Villas and Townhouses entry points are the "second door" and should grow into a small set of curated collections (by destination first, UK and UAE, and later by lifestyle) rather than a filter grid. Give the homepage a brand-led `<title>` and H1 (the serif headline "A place to call your own" with the eyebrow "Homes in the UK & UAE" already does this) and push keyword copy to catalogue and area pages. Add a "contact your advisor" block low on the page with a named person, a direct line and a stated response window, following E&V. Treat natural-language search as an open decision rather than a build item: it is now table stakes on the UAE portals, but on a curated catalogue of this size an inventory-backed autocomplete will serve the same need without a bot.

### Search and results: obey the filter rules, show counts, never dead-end

The usability evidence is unambiguous. Filters and results must be visible together, which on mobile means a tray or sheet on the same screen opened from a control labelled "Filter" rather than an icon, with a batch "Apply" button because batch filtering suits users with clear criteria and slower devices ([NN/g, mobile faceted search](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-faceted-search/); [NN/g, applying filters](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/applying-filters/)). An overview of applied filters above the results orients users, yet **28% of sites omit it**, and users who lose track of their filters "can make incorrect assumptions about the scope and incorrectly conclude that there are no suitable items available" ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/how-to-design-applied-filters)). Filters that silently return zero results are "a dead end" that the page must prevent or recover from ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-filter-ui)); **37% of sites fail to persist the search query** after submission ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/persist-search-queries)); no-results pages must say clearly that nothing matched, keep the query, and offer a way forward ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/search-no-results-serp/)); autocomplete suggestions that return zero or irrelevant results are worse than none ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/site-search-suggestions/)); and the default sort should be a diversity-based relevance, which **24% of sites get wrong** ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/default-sort-type)). Secondary reporting of Baymard's 2025 benchmark puts **78% of mobile filtering experiences at "poor to mediocre"** against 58% on desktop ([WisePIM](https://wisepim.com/blog/ecommerce-product-filters-ux-best-practices)). Google treats crawlable filter combinations as a duplicate-content problem and publishes specific guidance for faceted navigation ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/crawling-managing-faceted-navigation)).

The market conventions layer on top. UK buyers are trained by Rightmove to search by location with a radius in miles from 0 to 40, price band, bedrooms, property type, an "added to site" window and must-have keywords such as "garage" or "annexe" ([Apify Rightmove scraper docs](https://apify.com/longanimous_bracken/rightmove-scraper.md)); Zoopla adds an internal floor-area filter ([Zoopla support](https://support.zoopla.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/35136958444050)). UAE buyers expect completion status, furnishing and keyword filters, a "TruCheck First" verified-only control, sort by popular, latest, price and verified, and a clustered map whose round markers "display the number of available listings in that location" with its own building-and-community search bar ([Apify Bayut scraper docs](https://apify.com/khadinakbar/bayut-scraper.md); [Bayut App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id923263211); [MyBayut Map View guide](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/map-view-bayut-features-steps/)). Property Finder puts the live count in the page title ("Villas for Sale in Dubai - 15707 Houses for sale"), offers a "Verified" toggle, and since about September 2026 shows Community Reviews area ratings on results pages before a listing is opened ([Property Finder, villas for sale in Dubai](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/buy/dubai/villas-for-sale.html); [Property Finder blog, Verified Listings](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/propertyfinder-ae-sets-industry-benchmarks-with-the-release-of-verified-listings/); [Property Finder blog, Community Reviews](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/community-reviews-property-finder/)); Dubizzle offers "Show Verified First" ([Dubizzle](https://dubai.dubizzle.com/en/property-for-sale/off-plan/residential/)). For off-plan, Bayut's New Projects hub runs its own filter row, "Handover By, Payment Plan, % Completion", with list and map views ([bayut.com/new-projects](https://www.bayut.com/new-projects)). Among the luxury brokerages, Nest Seekers exposes a "luxury" tier as a path facet beside market and type and aggregates an "Ultra Luxury" collection, and its May 2026 app redesign added a card-and-grid viewing toggle, simpler filtering and faster map loading ([Prime Central London luxury](https://www.nestseekers.com/Sales/london-prime-central/luxury/); [Ultra Luxury](https://www.nestseekers.com/Properties/UltraLuxury/); [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nestseekersinternational.main&hl=en)). One caution on ordering: Bayut's results blend paid tiers with verification, since "Signature listings take the top spot, followed by hot then basic listings" and TruCheck boosts within each tier ([MyBayut Profolio feature page](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/new-features-checked-trucheck-smartleads-bayutpro/)), which a transparent brand should avoid or label.

**For Haus.** The catalogue's country, city, area, type, bedrooms and keyword filters are the right set for the current stock; what matters now is the mechanics. Make every filter count-aware so combinations with no stock are disabled or explained, show the matching count in the results heading and on the mobile "Apply" button, render applied filters as removable chips above the grid on both breakpoints with a one-tap "Clear all", persist the keyword in the field, and write an honest empty state that keeps the query and offers concrete widenings (another area, fewer bedrooms, register interest for future stock) rather than a decorative blank. Sort by relevance, newest and size only; a price sort would be dishonest while some stock legitimately carries no published price, and "popular" or "verified" orderings have no truthful basis on a curated showcase. Add UAE-only facets for completion status, handover window and payment plan only when the developer-published source carries those fields, which today it does not for Florence, so leave them out rather than approximate them. A map view and saved searches belong to the next phase: the first because map-only information fails accessibility unless it has a text twin, the second because an alert sign-up is a marketing-consent touchpoint that must be a separate, unticked choice.

### Community and project pages: tell the story through sourced facts, keep documents open

The developer sites share one information architecture: community page, then named project or collection, then unit type, then brochure and floor-plan downloads, with "Register your interest" as the universal primary call to action. Emaar's community pages list each sub-project "with brochure and floor plan links" and a starting price per card, and expose "View Brochure" directly rather than behind a form ([Emaar, The Oasis](https://www.emaar.com/en/our-communities/the-oasis); [Emaar, Emaar South](https://www.emaar.com/en/our-communities/emaar-south)); Nakheel's newer launch pages offer three open downloads, "Download Brochure", "Download Floor Plan", "Download Payment Plan", while its unpriced Palm Jebel Ali page carries only "REGISTER YOUR INTEREST" and "DISCOVER MORE" ([Nakheel, Palm Central Private Residences](https://www.nakheel.com/en/new-launches/palm-central-private-residences); [Nakheel, Palm Jebel Ali](https://www.nakheel.com/en/developments/nakheel-collections/palmjebelali)). Gating is a broker tactic: third-party landing pages for Emaar communities promise "the current price list, floor plans, payment plan and masterplan within 24 hours" in exchange for a form ([hsproperty.ae](https://hsproperty.ae/blogs/request-the-free-valley-by-emaar-brochure-and-floor-plans-2026everything-a-first-time-buyer-needs-in-one-pdf)). Azizi tells the Florence story through infrastructure rather than adjectives: "six gated clusters", a location line "along Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road (E311)", a named central park, a community mall described by its tenants, an on-site school and a "7,200+ villas & townhouses" counter, with the product mix stated as "3 and 4 bedroom townhouses, and 4, 5 and 6 bedroom villas" ([Azizi Florence project page](https://www.azizidevelopments.com/sharjah/um-fanain/azizi-florence); [Azizi, Um Fanain community page](https://www.azizidevelopments.com/sharjah/um-fanain)). Collections are branded by name and sequel (Emaar's Palmiera, Palmiera 2, Palmiera 3; Nakheel's "Beach Collection" and "Coral Collection" framed as "a limited collection of 44 beachfront villas"), and unit types are split product-first then by bedrooms, with Damac adding "end and mid units" and quoting sizes in sq m ([Nakheel press release, 20 Aug 2026](https://www.nakheel.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/news-detail/2026/08/20/nakheel-unveils-limited-collection-of-44-beachfront-villas-on-palm-jebel-ali-s-frond-f); [Villas for sale in DAMAC Islands 2](https://www.damacproperties.com/en/communities/damac-islands-2/for-sale/properties/villas/)). Construction progress is published as percentage milestones or a dedicated section ([Azizi press: Beach Oasis 80%](https://www.azizidevelopments.com/press/azizi-developments-beach-oasis-reaches-80-construction-milestone); [Nakheel, Jebel Ali Village construction progress](https://www.nakheel.com/en/construction-progress/jebel-ali-village)). Knight Frank, the brokerage with the clearest new-homes taxonomy, segments by stage, "off-plan, under-construction and completed schemes", and by "design-led and branded residences" ([Knight Frank New Homes](https://www.knightfrank.com/new-homes)).

The same sources show why provenance matters. Floor-plan PDFs carry tolerance disclaimers ("All room dimensions are measured to structural elements and exclude wall finishes and construction tolerances"; "the actual area may vary from the stated area") and brochures reserve the right to revise plans "without notice" ([Binghatti Hillviews floor plans](https://www2.binghatti.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Hillviews-Floorplans.pdf); [Sobha, The Element at Sobha One](https://sobharealty.com/media/qnbd1s2h/the-element-at-sobha-one.pdf); [Binghatti Royale brochure](https://www.binghatti.com/assets/upload/Brochure-Binghatti-Royale.pdf)). Multi-page estates drift: one Sobha page says every villa has a private pool while another says amenities vary ([Sobha Reserve campaign page](https://sobharealty.com/sobha-reserve-uae); [Sobha Reserve](https://sobharealty.com/properties-in-dubai/sobha-reserve)); Damac's own pages conflict on starting prices ([DAMAC Islands complete guide](https://www.damacproperties.com/en/blog/damac-islands-dubai-uae-complete-guide-0239/)); Bayut's Sharjah listings name a different developer from the Azizi brand and press reports disagree on Florence's unit counts and "from" prices ([Bayut, Sharjah Sustainable City II](https://www.bayut.com/for-sale/villas/sharjah/umm-fanain/sharjah-sustainable-city-ii/); [Khaleej Times, Sept 2026](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/azizi-announces-dh30-billion-sharjah-project-townhouse-prices-start-from-dh189-million); [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/business/uae-realty-market/8.1bln-azizi-florence-in-sharjah-to-feature-1130-villas-6000-townhouses-559529)). Developers hedge handover with "anticipated", "scheduled" or "expected" and a month-year or quarter; portals render it as "Delivery Date: Q3 2027" with a payment-plan count chip, or "Handover Q3 2028" beside an "80/20 payment plan" ([Nakheel press release, 11 Nov 2024](https://www.nakheel.com/en/media-centre/press-releases/news-detail/2024/11/11/nakheel-reveals-latest-release-of-exclusive-waterfront-villas-on-palm-jebel-ali); [Property Finder, new projects in Business Bay](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/new-projects/lp/dubai/business-bay); [Dubizzle, off-plan villas in Dubai](https://dubai.dubizzle.com/en/property-for-sale/off-plan/residential/villahouse/)). Dubai's advertising terms require an advert to state the project name, project number, location and the approved escrow account number and make the advertiser "responsible for the validity and credibility of the advertisement content" ([Dubai Land Department advertising terms (PDF, Arabic)](https://dubailand.gov.ae/media/daylptgk/الشروط-والاحكام-الخاصة-بالإعلانات-العقارية-1.pdf)), and DLD has fined three developers Dh500,000 each for marketing before registration ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/amp/business/property/dubai-land-department-fines-three-developers-dh15m-1.1717333796215)). Render disclaimers must be prominent enough that viewers are "instantly aware the image is a rendition, not the actual property", and a disclaimer "buried in an obscure section of a website offers little protection", so it belongs on every page that hosts the content ([BoxBrownie](https://www.boxbrownie.com/b/must-know-disclaimers-for-property-marketing); [AOE Team](https://www.aoeteam.com/post/best-practices-for-website-disclaimers)). A project microsite "should do one thing extremely well: help buyers understand the opportunity quickly enough to take the next step with confidence" ([Marketika](https://marketika.dev/insights/real-estate-project-microsite-checklist-for-developers)).

**For Haus.** The Florence build already exceeds the developer norm on the points that matter: named brochure designs that map to how Emaar and Nakheel brand collections, uncropped per-level plans, built-up area labelled with its brochure source, and withheld disputed plot figures. Keep that and add the provenance line as a visible design element, "Facts as published in the developer brochure dated …", on the community page, each collection and each design, because no benchmarked site does this and every one of them has been caught out by its own drift. Tell the community story the Azizi way, through sourced infrastructure facts, with amenities worded as "planned" unless completion is evidenced, and show no handover, payment plan or "from" price unless the developer publishes one, in the developer's own hedged wording and format. Label every render "illustration" in the frame, not the footer, and carry the floor-plan tolerance disclaimer under each uncropped plan. Keep brochures and plans open and the "Register your interest" form light, which matches Emaar and Nakheel and beats the broker gates. Because Florence is in Sharjah, any public advertising of it needs prior written approval from the Sharjah Real Estate Registration Department, which applies to brokers outside the emirate too ([Al Tamimi & Co](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/real-estate-development-in-the-emirate-of-sharjah/)); until that is in hand, the page should stay a clearly labelled showcase rather than an invitation to purchase.

### Listing detail: a gallery that hides nothing beside facts that are sourced

Baymard's testing of image galleries found that "time and again the UI and design of the image gallery… caused users to overlook the very images they were trying to seek out", and that **40% of sites truncate thumbnails** without indicating that more images exist, which led to direct abandonments ([Baymard](https://baymard.com/product-page/benchmark/page-designs/image-gallery-overlay/)). Bayut's detail page is the richest fact model in the set: a "Property Information" block with type, purpose, reference number, completion status, an absolute "Added on" date and, where relevant, TruCheck date and handover date; a "Features / Amenities" list with a "+ 4 more amenities" expander; a Floor Plans tab with 2D, 3D and rotatable "3D Live" plans; a Trends chart; a branded mortgage section on every sale listing since late 2025; and a regulatory block where "the DED number, RERA number and the Trakheesi Permit (Madmoun Service) QR code appear" ([Bayut listing](https://www.bayut.com/property/details-15650264.html); [MyBayut 3D Live](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/3d-live-bayut-feature); [Khaleej Times, 26 Nov 2025](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/uae-homebuyers-check-monthly-mortgage-while-browsing-bayut-dubizzle-prypco); [Bayut agent portal, Madmoun guide](https://www.bayut.com/agentportal/agents-guide-to-dld-madmoun-service/)). Its distinctive habit is that almost every block names its source: a DLD-validated "Property Overview" appears only on Checked listings, and transaction data is presented "without changing the transaction values" ([Bayut agent portal](https://www.bayut.com/agentportal/?p=9088); [Bayut support](https://support.bayut.com/hc/en-us/articles/35546819623186-What-is-the-source-of-the-Dubai-Transactions-data)).

The luxury brokerages set the price and media conventions. Knight Frank and Savills show international stock in local currency with a sterling conversion in brackets, "£4,173,503 (€4,995,000)" and "Fixed price €390,000 (£325,092)", use a qualifier vocabulary of guide price, fixed price and price on application, and host per-property PDF brochures with language variants ([Zoopla branch page: Knight Frank International](https://legacy.zoopla.co.uk/find-agents/branch/knight-frank-international-london-56196); [Savills property detail](https://search.savills.com/gr/en/property-detail/sz10897978); [Knight Frank brochure PDF](https://content.knightfrank.com/property/str012366501/brochures/en/str012366501-en-brochure-6b5cb0e6-8d4b-4712-9f4b-7774abd75541-1.pdf)). Sotheby's lets buyers text an agent from every mobile listing page and converts prices into about 60 currencies; Elliman's platform offered 55 ([Inman, 19 Jun 2020](https://www.inman.com/2020/06/19/sothebys-international-realty-launches-4-new-website-features/); [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/sothebys-international-realty-triumphs-in-the-25th-annual-webby-awards-2021-05-19); [PR Newswire, 6 Mar 2020](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/the-next-move-is-yours-douglas-elliman-unveils-new-website-new-branding-and-new-tagline-and-logo-301019040.html)). Knight Frank's app delivers galleries, floor plans and downloadable brochures and books viewings "in person or virtually" ([App Store (GB)](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/knight-frank-property/id350149855)); E&V's exposé standard is "meaningful descriptions, high-quality photos, 3D tours and clear floor plans" with "Price on request" as an accepted state ([E&V, Sell Property](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/en-de/sell-property/); [Zug shop](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ch/en/shops/zug)). Zoopla reports that adding floor plans lifted search clicks by **9% for sales** ([Zoopla support](https://support.zoopla.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/360004963738)), and a professionally measured plan is recommended over a scan-derived one "where official square footage matters" ([Open Homes](https://www.open-homes.com/blog/which-floor-plan-is-right-for-your-listing-a-guide-for-bay-area-real-estate-agents/)). Nest Seekers shows the cost of loose formatting: facts blocks with "724.0 to 1545.0 sq ft", agent-written ALL-CAPS titles, dollar and euro conversions that "differ between pages for the same property", and a sold home still labelled "For Sale: Guide Price" ([Brooklyn new-development listing](https://www.nestseekers.com/3769725/228-13th-street-228-nyc-ny-11215-from-1-to-3-br-condo); [Palm Jumeirah penthouse listing](https://www.nestseekers.com/3252094/one-crescent-dubai-united-arab-emirates-6-br-penthouse); [Greenwich castle listing](https://www.nestseekers.com/1675633/4-br-castle-london-greater-london-united-kingdom)). On UK facts, Rightmove's template now shows council tax band, tenure with years remaining, ground rent and service charge, broadband in Ofcom tiers and mobile signal by operator ([Rightmove listing](https://www.rightmove.co.uk/properties/173138879)), the EPC rating must appear in any advertisement where a certificate exists ([Just Landlords guidance](https://justlandlords.co.uk/?p=10589)), and "price on application" withholds the asking price in breach of consumer law, which is why Zoopla dropped it ([NTS](https://nationaltradingstandards.uk/news/price-on-application-in-property-listings-deemed-unlawful-as-national-trading-standards-publishes-opinion); [Zoopla](https://www.zoopla.co.uk/discover/property-news/price-on-application-to-be-dropped-from-property-listings)). Missing alt text on listing photos and inaccessible virtual tours are among the most-cited accessibility complaints against property sites ([Edensign](https://edensign.io/blog/ada-and-web-accessibility-for-real-estate-websites-2026-wcag-section-508-listing-photo-alt-text)).

**For Haus.** Put the gallery and the facts block in the same first viewport on both breakpoints, with a visible image count ("1 of 24", "View all photos") so nothing is hidden, and keep the facts strip inline and quiet in the current card style: price with its qualifier, location, bedrooms and bathrooms, area with its basis and both units, tenure or completion status, and a reference number. For the North London home the public asking price, the approximate gross internal floor area and the labelled BEFORE/AFTER interior concepts already follow the evidence; add tenure, council tax band and the EPC rating as soon as they are known, and label the concepts as illustrations in-frame. Show a second currency only as a clearly marked indicative conversion from one daily rate, never two figures that can disagree. Use progressive disclosure the Bayut way, a short amenities preview with "+N more" and a floor-plans tab, so the page stays visually calm. Offer three honest, equal actions, request a viewing, enquire and open the brochure, with no countdowns, "x people viewing" or scarcity prompts, and let status badges reflect live data with separate sold and withdrawn templates. Write human alt text for every photograph and give each floor plan a text equivalent of its rooms and dimensions.

### Enquiry and brochure flows: a trade, not a gate, with consent kept separate

The luxury convention is agent-first contact. Savills pages carry a "Contact now" button beside each named agent and point buyers to a downloadable PDF brochure or the selling agents for a copy, with viewings "strictly by appointment" ([Savills property detail](https://search.savills.com/gr/en/property-detail/nzl0ca044768l); [Savills property detail](https://search.savills.com/property-detail/gbyoruyor250038)); Knight Frank listings name their own contacts with phone and email and their brochures state they are not an offer or contract ([Knight Frank brochure PDF](https://content.knightfrank.com/property/kekn0040451/brochures/en/kekn0040451-en-brochure-246ee612-6e31-40ee-9241-b79217a6ff3f-1.pdf)); Nest Seekers asks buyers to "contact the agent to schedule a private showing" and offers virtual tours on request, with no brochure download visible, a lead-capture tactic that weakens the self-serve experience ([Olympic Tower penthouse listing](https://www.nestseekers.com/3597442/olympic-tower-641-ph45-manhattan-ny-10022-8-br-condo); [West Village listing](https://www.nestseekers.com/1695344/70-charlton-street-virtual-tours-of-70-charlton-residences-are-now)). Hafren, at the other end of the market, routes viewings to an email address and a weekday-only office ([Hafren listing](https://www.hafrenproperties.co.uk/property/275000-investment-opportunity-mixed-use-property-comprising-ground-floor-commercial-unit-and-spacious-three-bedroom-maisonette-with-garden-in-penarth-road-grangetown-cardiff-cf11-6nj/)). The 2026 trend literature is explicit that "aggressive gating is out" in favour of a "concierge approach" with a short invitation and a simple form, because luxury buyers "expect a trade, not a gate" ([Placester](https://placester.com/real-estate-marketing-academy/10-real-estate-website-design-trends-in-2026); [DMR Media](https://www.dmrmedia.org/blog/Real-Estate-Website-Design-Trends)). Bayut publishes the service standard it expects of agents, a "Responsive Broker" badge for a response rate of 70% or higher measured "during business hours (09:00 am to 09:00 pm on weekdays)" ([Bayut support](https://support.bayut.com/hc/en-us/articles/20837115219218-What-is-a-TruBroker)), and lists phone, email, WhatsApp and live chat as agent channels ([MyBayut Bayut Chat](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/bayut-chat-feature)). Compass's redesigned seller disclosure, which states what the seller forgoes in each marketing phase, is a documented example of transparency built into a flow ([Compass press release](https://www.compass.com/newsroom/press-releases/7GTGPrZsN0vVh15XkhDnWo/)).

The form and consent rules are settled. The ICO requires PECR consent to cover both the specific organisation and the communication type, and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 changes have put parts of its direct-marketing guidance "under review" ([ICO, direct marketing using electronic mail](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-direct-marketing-using-electronic-mail/); [ICO, choosing your lawful basis](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/sending-direct-marketing-choosing-your-lawful-basis/)); valid consent is a clear affirmative action, so pre-ticked boxes do not count, requests must be granular and as easy to withdraw as to give ([ICO, What is valid consent?](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/consent/what-is-valid-consent/)); the ICO finalised its cookie guidance on 29 April 2026 ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guidance-on-the-use-of-storage-and-access-technologies/)). GDPR practice summaries agree a contact form used only to answer an enquiry can rest on legitimate interest while marketing needs separate, never pre-ticked consent with versioned records of the wording in use ([Legiscope, 2026](https://www.legiscope.com/blog/examples-of-gdpr-consent.html)). GOV.UK's pattern is to validate on submit, show an error summary at the top, move focus to it and repeat each message inline ([GOV.UK Design System, Error summary](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-summary/)); Baymard adds that inline validation should flag after the user leaves a field, not while typing, and that required and optional fields should both be marked ([Baymard, Inline Form Validation](https://baymard.com/blog/inline-form-validation); [Baymard, Required vs Optional fields](https://baymard.com/blog/required-optional-form-fields)). In the UAE, Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 has been in force since January 2022 but 2026 sources still describe its executive regulations as unissued, so documented, consent-based processing is the prudent baseline ([Business Dubai analysis](https://www.businessdubai.ae/blogs/uae-pdpl-data-protection); [Orentara](https://orentara.com/en/insights/uae-pdpl-executive-regulations-status)).

**For Haus.** The simulated brochure request with its example email and its optional, unticked marketing consent is already the compliant shape; keep the "simulated" label prominent until the flow is live. Hold the form to four to six fields (name, email, phone optional unless a call-back is requested, message or preferred time), mark required and optional explicitly, validate on submit with a focused error summary and inline messages that preserve input, and deliver the brochure whether or not the consent box is ticked so that consent is never a condition of access. Offer one unticked checkbox per channel (email; SMS or WhatsApp) worded for Haus of Estate specifically, a one-line privacy notice at the point of capture, and a stated response window on the advisor card, which is the boutique equivalent of Bayut's published 70% standard. Add WhatsApp and call links on every listing for UAE buyers. Before building a "request a call" flow for UAE leads, verify the 2024 telemarketing resolution, which the research could not source. A structured "arrange a viewing" request with preferred dates and in-person or virtual choice is the natural next step and would exceed both Hafren and the Nest Seekers "on request" pattern.

### Trust and compliance: render the legal duties as design elements

Trust on the benchmarked sites is a stack of market-specific legal signals plus brand proof. In the UK, the Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations were replaced on 6 April 2025 by Part 4 Chapter 1 of the DMCC Act 2024, enforced directly by the CMA with penalties of up to **10% of global turnover** ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/int/legal-updates/the-dmcc-act-consumer-elements-come-into-force-from-6-april-2025)); the NTSELAT material-information guidance was withdrawn on 8 May 2025 but the duty to disclose survives ([Propertymark](https://www.propertymark.co.uk/resource/material-information-guidance-withdrawn-as-the-digital-markets-competition-and-consumers-act-takes-over.html); [Today's Conveyancer](https://todaysconveyancer.co.uk/ntselat-material-information-guidance-withdrawn-but-duty-remains/)); MHCLG consulted on replacement guidance from 6 October to 29 December 2025 and the June 2026 reform roadmap promised it "later this year", with none found by 6 November 2026 ([MHCLG consultation hub](https://consult.communities.gov.uk/home-buying-and-selling/material-information-in-property-listings/consultation/subpage.2025-09-23.6163940232); [Homeowners Passport](https://www.homeownerspassport.com/knowledge-hub/home-buying-selling-reform-roadmap-2026/)). A Reapit survey found **46% of agents still using the withdrawn Parts A, B and C lists** and 40% unsure ([Property Industry Eye](https://propertyindustryeye.com/most-agents-feel-unsupported-amid-material-information-change/)), so those lists, price or rent, tenure and council tax (Part A), utilities, rooms and parking (Part B), flood risk, covenants, building safety and planning where relevant (Part C), remain the working template ([Propertymark summary](https://www.propertymark.co.uk/resource/full-material-information-guidance-published.html)). The CMA's final price transparency guidance requires the total price including mandatory charges in every invitation to purchase and bans drip pricing ([Reed Smith](https://www.reedsmith.com/articles/full-price-no-surprises-cma-s-final-price-transparency-guidance-arrives/)). Agents must belong to The Property Ombudsman or the Property Redress Scheme ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/lettings-agents-and-property-managers-redress-schemes)); letting agents in England must publish a client money protection certificate on their website, with penalties up to £30,000 for non-membership ([Propertymark](https://www.propertymark.co.uk/resource/cmp-becomes-a-legal-requirement-in-england.html)), and a fee list with redress and CMP statements ([Consumer Rights Act 2015 s.83](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2015/15/section/83)); from 1 May 2026 the Renters' Rights Act requires a specific advertised rent and bans inviting higher offers ([LexisNexis](https://www.lexisnexis.com/en-gb/legal/guidance/the-renters-rights-act-2025-rent-changes-transparency-in-the-private-rental-sector-in-england); [Trowers & Hamlins](https://www.trowers.com/insights/2025/july/renters-rights-bill-rental-bidding)). The TPO code for sales agents was re-issued in March 2026 ([TPO sales code PDF](https://www.tpos.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/TPOE27-9_A5-Code-of-Practice-for-Residential-Estate-Agents-A5-March-2026.pdf)). A Cardiff company serving Wales must also account for Rent Smart Wales licensing, Welsh LTT bands that differ from SDLT (and on which calculator sites disagree), and a reinvented EPC held back to 2027 ([Pembrokeshire Trading Standards](https://cms.pembrokeshire.gov.uk/trading-standards-information/landlords-letting-agents-property-management); [BritCalc](https://britcalc.pages.dev/sdlt-calc); [Propertymark](https://www.propertymark.co.uk/resource/anticipated-reinvention-of-epcs-held-back-until-2027.html)). The Equality Act's duty to make reasonable adjustments is anticipatory and applies to websites as services ([EHRC Services Code of Practice](https://www.equalityhumanrights.com/guidance/services-public-functions-and-associations-statutory-code-practice)).

In the UAE, every Dubai advert needs a Trakheesi permit and DLD states "all real estate companies are required to display the QR code" from the Madmoun service on all visual and written adverts, with an AI-enabled governance platform added in April 2025 ([DLD news release](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/news-media/dubai-land-department-strengthens-transparency-with-ai-enabled-real-estate-advertising-governance/)); scanning the code opens the official permit card showing the advertiser, the property and whether it has been sold or rented ([Dubai Media Office, 18 Apr 2023](https://mediaoffice.ae/en/news/2023/April/18-04/Dubai-Land-department-barcode-check)). Abu Dhabi's "Madhmoun" permit became mandatory on 7 July 2025 ([Aletihad](https://en.aletihad.ae/news/business/4588598/madhmoun-permit-system-comes-into-force--aims-to-promote-tra)); Sharjah requires prior written SRERD approval and its economic department requires advertising materials in Arabic ([Al Tamimi & Co](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/real-estate-development-in-the-emirate-of-sharjah/); [SEDD](https://sedd.ae/en/web/sedd/home)). Broker sources add that off-plan adverts must show the developer name, escrow account number, expected completion date and proposed service fees ([PropSpace](https://www.propspace.com/blog/uae-real-estate-advertising-permits)), though the primary field list was not verified. The portals then layer their own badges: Bayut's "Checked" (documents verified against DLD) and time-stamped TruCheck (a live on-site photo, valid 30 days for rentals and 45 for sales, with "Expiring Soon" at seven days) and the TruBroker agent badge held by more than 5,700 agents by October 2025 ([Bayut agent portal, TruCheck guide](https://www.bayut.com/agentportal/heres-your-guide-to-the-trucheck-badge/); [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/property/trubrokers-first-anniversary-a-new-era-of-credibility-in-uaes-property-market-1.500322869)); Property Finder's monthly-refreshed SuperAgent badge requires an approved transaction in the last 90 days and a customer rating of 4.0 or higher ([Property Finder help centre](https://support.propertyfinder.ae/hc/en-us/articles/24558432104722)). Brand-level proof is weaker where copy is not centrally managed: Nest Seekers' live pages claim both "80 offices" and "30 offices" ([Why Nest Seekers](https://www.nestseekers.com/Company/WhyNestSeekers); [International offices](https://www.nestseekers.com/Offices/international)), Hafren's trust rests on a UKALA mention and a 4.9/5 GetAgent score from 33 reviews ([OnTheMarket branch page](https://onthemarket.com/agents/branch/hafren-properties-grangetown); [GetAgent](https://www.getagent.co.uk/branch/hafren-properties-cf11/95880)), and Compass's private-listing strategy drew public criticism that forced a redesigned disclosure ([Boston.com, May 2025](https://www.boston.com/real-estate/real-estate-news/2025/05/22/compass-real-estate-criticized-marketing-strategy/)). Web-accessibility suits reached about 3,117 in 2025, up 27%, and the FTC's April 2025 order against accessiBe discredited overlay widgets ([Edensign](https://edensign.io/blog/ada-and-web-accessibility-for-real-estate-websites-2026-wcag-section-508-listing-photo-alt-text)).

**For Haus.** Build a quiet compliance strip into the footer that reads as part of the typography rather than as legal clutter: HAUS OF ESTATE LTD with its company number, registered office in Cardiff and place of registration, email and any VAT number, the redress scheme with membership number and logo, the ICO registration, and links to privacy, cookies, an accessibility statement and a complaints procedure; add the CMP certificate and fee list the day lettings begin. On each UK listing carry the Part A facts in the headline block, Part B in a key-facts table, Part C as declared flags, and a "Material information last reviewed on" date, and never use POA. For UAE stock, model a per-emirate permit object (authority, number, expiry, QR URL) that blocks publication when missing or expired, show the brokerage ORN and agent BRN beside the advisor card, and mirror the Dubai advert elements on Sharjah pages once SRERD approval exists. Where a property is confirmed by Haus rather than by a portal, use honest wording, "agent-confirmed on [date]", because the TruCheck model is agent-performed and reviewed, not independently verified. Feed every footprint figure (offices, team, markets) from one data source so the site never contradicts itself the way Nest Seekers does.

### Content: dated snapshots, sourced area facts, and people presented as the product

Every leader runs an owned editorial and research layer that doubles as a trust and search engine. Knight Frank offers The Wealth Report 2026, its twentieth edition, as a download and in full online with regional microsites ([Knight Frank Wealth Report](https://www.knightfrank.com/research/reports/wealthreport)); Christie's Luxury Defined blog reached more than 37,000 email subscribers and its relaunched 200-page magazine pairs topic-led features with property spotlights ([InPublishing](https://inpublishing.co.uk/articles/august-creates-lifestyle-blog-for-christies-5791); [InPublishing, magazine makeover](https://inpublishing.co.uk/articles/christies-property-magazine-gets-makeover-9964)); Engel & Völkers' GG magazine runs online in five languages mixing editorial with network listings ([PRWeb](https://www.prweb.com/releases/engel_v_lkers_launches_international_website_of_the_property_and_lifestyle_magazine_gg_/prweb11229318.htm)); Elliman added a "World of Elliman" lifestyle hub in 2025 ([Inman, 9 Apr 2025](https://www.inman.com/2025/04/09/douglas-elliman-launches-new-ai-powered-website/)). The portals build the same authority from data: Bayut's area guides carry rent and sale trend tables by unit type with explicit empty states such as "Information for average price is currently not available", and its building guides list floors, unit counts, ownership, developer and completion year from DLD data "after cleanup to remove outliers" ([Bayut Area Guide, Indigo Ville 6](https://bayut.com/area-guides/indigo-ville-6); [Bayut buildings page](https://bayut.com/buildings/clone-bg)); Property Finder's area-insights pages add a map of amenities and community price data "based on Property Finder's last 12 months' listing data" ([Property Finder, Palm Jumeirah area guide](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/area-insights/dubai/palm-jumeirah); [Property Finder, Green Community villas](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/buy/dubai/villas-for-sale-dubai-investment-park-dip-dubai-investment-park-1-dip-1-green-community.html)). Nest Seekers illustrates both the right and the wrong way to keep content fresh: its Manhattan rentals page carries dated commentary ("as of Spring 2026, approximately 20.6% of Manhattan rentals… include concessions") while its market-report library's newest item dates from February 2023 ([Manhattan rentals](https://www.nestseekers.com/Rentals/manhattan/); [February 2023 Ultra Luxury update](https://www.nestseekers.com/Guides/MarketReports/february-ultra-luxury-market-update-2023/)). Hafren's "Cardiff Bay" page is an archive of listings rather than a guide with schools, transport and lifestyle content ([Cardiff Bay](https://www.hafrenproperties.co.uk/property-area/cardiff-bay/)).

People pages follow the same logic. Nest Seekers lets buyers browse agents by market and by language, with profiles that foreground fluency and licence titles, and office pages that name a local leader, a street address and a local mailbox rather than a generic inbox ([French-speaking agents](https://nestseekers.com/Company/Language/french); [Dubai office](https://www.nestseekers.com/Offices/nest-seekers-dubai)); E&V shop pages list advisors with direct numbers ([Ottawa advisors](https://www.evrealestate.com/en/shops/ottawa/our-shops/ottawa/our-advisors)). Luxury bio guidance stresses a specific track record, the professional network served and a confidentiality commitment, and holds that one strong testimonial beats three generic ones ([Propfade](https://propfade.com/real-estate-agent-branding/luxury-agent-bio-examples); [The Close](https://theclose.com/write-real-estate-agent-bio/)). Localisation is where the international brands compete on breadth: Sotheby's offered 14 to 15 human-translated languages and about 60 currencies, and Christie's translates descriptions into 19 languages ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/sothebys-international-realty-triumphs-in-the-25th-annual-webby-awards-2021-05-19); [Christie's listing presentation](https://brochures.christiesrealestate.com/view/407982236)), whereas Bayut, despite many site languages, offers no native currency or unit toggle, which is why a third-party Chrome extension exists to "convert Bayut property prices other currencies, calculate sqm" ([Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/bayut-property-indicators/hcchnedjfflpileimoinmlhiockbcfij)). Formatting conventions diverge even within one market, with portals writing "AED 2M", Emaar "AED 4.39M", Azizi "AED 2.09 M" and the press "Dh1.89 million", and the new dirham symbol unveiled on 27 March 2025 has no Unicode code point, so web display needs a custom glyph with an "AED" fallback ([Dubizzle, off-plan villas under AED 2M](https://dubai.dubizzle.com/en/property-for-sale/residential/p/off-plan-villas-for-sale-under-2000000-dubai/); [Gulf News, 27 Mar 2025](https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/new-symbol-revealed-for-uaes-dirham-in-physical-digital-forms-1.500074540); [Gulf News, how to use the new symbol](https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/how-to-use-the-new-uae-dirham-symbol-the-right-way-spot-incorrect-use-1.500160926)).

**For Haus.** Publish few things and date them: a small named journal, one dated market snapshot per market (UK prime and Sharjah or Dubai), and area pages for Umm Fanain and North London built from sourced facts with Bayut-style honest empty states where data is thin. Give the team page the Nest Seekers filters of market and language, add registration numbers where they exist, and keep office pages physical (address, local line, named lead). Write the formatting rules into the design system now: store AED and GBP as ISO codes, show sq ft with sq m beside it, no stray decimals, sentence-case human-written titles with the address or design name first, and one daily FX rate labelled "approx." wherever a second currency appears. Reserve Arabic and the new dirham glyph for the locale work in a later phase.

### Performance and accessibility: budget the hero, honour reduced motion, exceed AA

The Core Web Vitals thresholds are unchanged in 2026: **LCP at or under 2.5 s, INP at or under 200 ms and CLS at or under 0.1**, judged at the 75th percentile of real Chrome users over 28 days, with INP having replaced FID in March 2024 ([web.dev, Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals); [blog.mean.ceo, November 2026 round-up](https://blog.mean.ceo/core-web-vitals-news-november-2026/)). On a photo-led page the LCP element is the hero, so it must be discoverable in the HTML, carry `fetchpriority="high"` and never be lazy-loaded, which about 17% of pages still get wrong ([web.dev, Optimize LCP](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-lcp); [CoreWebVitals.io](https://www.corewebvitals.io/pagespeed/fix-slow-hero-images-core-web-vitals)); WebP should be the default with AVIF for heroes, "40–60% smaller than JPEG at similar quality" but heavier to decode on low-end devices ([Sammapix, 2026](https://www.sammapix.com/blog/optimize-images-core-web-vitals-2026)); images, embeds and map slots need reserved dimensions and fonts need preloading with metric-matched fallbacks to avoid layout shift ([web.dev, Optimize CLS](https://web.dev/articles/optimize-cls); [web.dev, font best practices](https://web.dev/articles/font-best-practices)). The benchmark's cautionary case is Nest Seekers, whose technology profile listed 24 payment and currency tools, 11 social, 7 advertising and 7 analytics technologies, and whose own 2026 app notes headline "faster screen and map loading" as the improvement ([SimilarWeb](https://www.similarweb.com/fr/company/nestseekers.com/); [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.nestseekersinternational.main&hl=en)); the leaders' documented commitments are mobile-first design, server-rendered listing pages and shared design systems ([Apify, E&V scraper](https://apify.com/recordsdata/engelvoelkers-scraper); [Compass True North](https://medium.com/compass-true-north/designing-parts-of-a-design-system-17aabd5b0644)).

On accessibility, WCAG 2.2 (October 2023) added Target Size (Minimum) at Level AA with **24×24 CSS px**, keeping 44×44 as the AAA enhanced level, plus Focus Not Obscured (Minimum) at AA, Focus Appearance at AAA (a focus indicator at least as large as a 2 px perimeter with 3:1 change), and Redundant Entry for forms ([W3C, What's New in WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/); [W3C Understanding SC 2.5.8](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html); [W3C Understanding 2.4.13](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/focus-appearance.html)). Content must reflow at 320 CSS px without two-dimensional scrolling except for maps and large images, any motion that starts automatically and lasts more than five seconds needs a pause control, and interaction-triggered animation should respect `prefers-reduced-motion` ([W3C Understanding 1.4.10](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html); [W3C Understanding 2.2.2](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/pause-stop-hide.html); [W3C Understanding 2.3.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/animation-from-interactions.html)). The WebAIM Million audit found roughly **95% of home pages with detectable failures**, led by low-contrast text (about 79%), missing alternative text (about 56%) and missing form labels (about 48%), figures the researchers recalled from the 2025 edition and marked for re-checking ([WebAIM Million](https://webaim.org/projects/million/)). Christie's Dubai affiliate site was briefed to be accessible "for all people regardless of their disabilities", the only such commitment documented among the brands ([Digital Gravity](https://www.digitalgravity.ae/case-studies/website/real-estate/christies/)). For search engines, schema.org's RealEstateListing type exists but Google offers no rich result for it, so markup is for accuracy and must match the visible page ([Haute Living](https://hauteliving.com/realestate/insights/real-estate-listing-schema); [Google Search Central, structured data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies)), and a UK/UAE site should serve locale URLs with bidirectional hreflang (en-GB, en-AE, ar-AE, x-default), self-referencing canonicals and no IP-based redirects ([Google Search Central, localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions); [Google Search Central, multi-regional sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites)).

**For Haus.** The 44 px targets, visible focus and reduced-motion handling already exceed WCAG 2.2 AA; make the focus commitment measurable by adopting the 2.4.13 formula as the house style and testing that the white sticky header, any cookie banner and a future floating enquiry button never obscure focus. Set the performance budget per template (home, catalogue, community, design, listing) at a 390 px viewport on a throttled connection: one eager hero sized to its rendered width in AVIF or WebP with a JPEG fallback, every other image lazy-loaded inside a fixed aspect box, Cormorant Garamond and Inter preloaded as subset WOFF2 with fallback metrics, third-party scripts deferred and counted, and real-user monitoring from launch. Put a measured scrim behind any text over the villa hero and test it against the lightest and darkest photographs. Make galleries and the floor-plan viewer keyboard-operable with named controls, publish tagged PDFs or HTML brochures, write an accessibility statement with a contact route, and emit RealEstateListing, Offer, Accommodation, RealEstateAgent and BreadcrumbList JSON-LD whose values are identical to the visible page, with Offer availability following live status so the markup can never claim what the page does not.

## Roadmap: now, next, later

The sequence below orders the recommendations by how much they protect the brand's credibility per unit of effort, which is also the order in which the regulators and the leaders reward them. "Now" items can land on the current review branch without new data; "Next" items need real stock data, legal confirmation or a locale decision; "Later" items are the platform features the leaders use to retain clients and should wait until the catalogue justifies them.

## Open decisions the owner and designer should settle

Several choices cannot be made from the evidence alone because they are matters of positioning, and the benchmark shows credible brands on both sides. The first is pricing posture for Florence: the evidence says show a specific price wherever a page is an invitation to purchase, and Nakheel's unpriced, register-interest-only page is the only honest alternative; the owner must decide whether Florence pages are an advertisement (which triggers SRERD approval and the price duty) or a showcase of a developer's published collection, and the page copy must then say which ([Nakheel, Palm Jebel Ali](https://www.nakheel.com/en/developments/nakheel-collections/palmjebelali); [NTS](https://nationaltradingstandards.uk/news/price-on-application-in-property-listings-deemed-unlawful-as-national-trading-standards-publishes-opinion)). The second is natural-language search, which Bayut now calls a complement to filters and E&V places on its homepage, against trend advice that luxury buyers reject scripted bots; the recommendation is to decide after the catalogue has enough stock for an inventory-backed autocomplete to feel insufficient ([Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/bayut-introduces-ai-powered-property-search-to-transform-the-way-consumers-find-their-next-home-469171); [Placester](https://placester.com/real-estate-marketing-academy/10-real-estate-website-design-trends-in-2026)). The third is the brochure trade-off: Savills offers direct download, developers publish openly, brokers gate; the evidence favours open documents with optional capture, but the owner should confirm that lead volume is not the showcase's purpose ([Savills property detail](https://search.savills.com/property-detail/gbyoruyor250038)). The fourth is the valuation stance for UK sellers, where Strutt & Parker branches offer an instant estimate with follow-up while Hamptons argues "people give better property advice than instant valuations"; a luxury brand can take either position but should take one deliberately ([Strutt & Parker branch page](https://struttandparker.com/local/estate-agents-in-ipswich/ipswich-1); [Hamptons](https://www.hamptons.co.uk/services/expert-property-valuation)). The fifth is locale architecture, country-language folders in the E&V and Savills pattern against a single global shell with in-page switches in the SIR pattern; the researchers' view, which this report shares, is that /uk and /ae folders give each market its own canonical page and regulatory footer ([E&V DE/EN](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/de/en); [savills.com](https://www.savills.com/)). The sixth is scope for the account layer and whether shared shortlists are worth building before there is a team to respond to comments. The seventh is the "100xlab" reference, which should be confirmed or dropped. Two research gaps also need closing before build decisions depend on them: the final MHCLG material-information guidance, which may have been published after the June 2026 roadmap, and the primary DLD field list for off-plan adverts, which the notes could only reconstruct from broker blogs.

## Conclusion

The most useful thing this benchmark reveals is that the luxury feel at the top of the market is manufactured upstream of the website, in photography standards, render studios and owned media, and then distributed through layouts that get out of the way; the websites themselves, read closely, are full of the small failures that undermine premium positioning, from decimals and all-caps titles to contradictory office counts, stale sold labels and pages that disagree with their own brochures. A brand without a television series or a 1,100-office network cannot borrow that upstream authority, but it can out-execute the leaders on exactly the discipline they neglect, and the direction chosen on 6 November gives it the visual foundation to do so.

That reframes Haus of Estate's hard constraints. Rules written to avoid invented prices, availability, handover dates and completed-facility claims look, from inside a product team, like limits on what the site can say; seen against the DMCC Act's automatic unfairness test, the CMA's price-transparency enforcement, DLD's AI-monitored advert governance and the Renters' Rights Act's fixed-rent rule, they are a description of where every serious operator in both markets will have to end up. Building provenance, open documents and compliance into the design language now, while the catalogue is small, costs little and means the brighter site will still be telling the truth when it is large.
