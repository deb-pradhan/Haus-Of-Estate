# Lead enquiry notice snapshot: 2026-09-01.v2

This is the immutable wording record for lead form `2026-09-01.v4` and privacy
notice `2026-09-01.v2`. Create a new snapshot instead of editing this file when
the displayed wording changes.

## Required acknowledgement

> I have read the Privacy Policy and understand how Haus of Estate will use my
> details to respond to this request.

Supporting text:

> This acknowledgement is required to continue. It does not subscribe you to
> marketing. Read the Privacy Policy.

The acknowledgement records that the notice was presented. Haus does not rely
on it as consent for handling a requested enquiry. The privacy policy identifies
steps taken at the person's request and legitimate interests as the relevant
lawful bases, subject to Haus's final legal review.

## Optional newsletter consent

## Submission notice

> By selecting "Send my brief", you ask Haus of Estate to use the details you
> provided to handle this request. This is separate from optional email
> marketing. Read our privacy policy.

The button name changes to "Send enquiry" or "Subscribe" where the selected
interest requires it.

## Country preference

The form labels the existing `preferences.market` field as "Country of
interest" and offers United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, Indonesia, Cyprus,
and Other / not sure. This is a property preference only. It is not used to
infer nationality, residence, applicable privacy law, or marketing permission.
