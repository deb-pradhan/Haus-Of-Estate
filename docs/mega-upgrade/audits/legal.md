Audited 18 Oct 2026, read-only. Not legal advice: items marked COUNSEL need a solicitor to confirm scope before publishing.
Legend: **ENG** = engineer can do now in code. **FOUNDER** = needs a fact/document only the founder holds. Never invent these; use the PLACEHOLDER markers below.
