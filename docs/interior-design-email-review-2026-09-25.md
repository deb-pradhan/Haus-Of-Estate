## Commercial details that remain proposals

Afifa's 17 September PDF suggests launch/standard **design fees** of £149/£250 for consultation, £499/£750 per room for Christmas Home Edit, £999/£1,500 per room for Signature Room Design, kitchen design from £1,499/£2,000, bathroom design from £999/£1,500, and bespoke full-home quotations. Furniture, materials, trades and installation are excluded from those fees. Separate suggested per-room material/furniture budgets are Essential £3,000-£8,000, Premium £8,000-£20,000 and Luxury £20,000+. These are internal source facts, not publishable Haus price commitments.

Package language is inconsistent across proposals: Essential/Complete/Signature; quality tiers Essential/Standard/Premium; budget tiers Essential/Premium/Luxury; Consult/Design/Renovate/Complete; Consultation/Refresh/Transformation/Turnkey; and Basic/Standard/Premium. They describe different concepts and must not be merged into one invented offer. Christmas versus a general launch also remains undecided in the reviewed material.
