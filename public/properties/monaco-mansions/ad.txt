# Haus of Estate — Development Notes

## Header & Navigation

- **Two-tier header**: contact strip on top (phone, WhatsApp, email, "Speak to an advisor") above four nav items — Properties, Services, Insights, About
- **Two regional phones**:
  - UAE +971 58 560 7033
  - UK +44 7496 033321
- **Services menu** trimmed to specialist work only: Property Management, Staging, Furnishing, Renovations
- **About menu** groups About us, Our team, Careers and FAQs in one quiet drop-down
- **"Sign In" button removed** — we weren't running a member area, so the button was a promise we couldn't keep
- **Mobile menu** rebuilt with collapsible groups instead of a long crammed list

## Hero Search (That Actually Searches)

- Buy / Rent tabs, with location, property type and bedrooms aligned to what we genuinely offer
- "Find my match" takes the visitor to the live catalogue with their filters applied
- Filter chips above the results — dismiss one or clear them all
- No match? A calm card invites the visitor to let an advisor find them an off-market option, replied to within two working hours
- Selling? A separate "Get a free valuation" link sits beneath the search, where it belongs

## Property Catalogue

- One showcase per development — no per-unit listings, no public unit numbers
- Azizi Monaco Mansions (Dubai South) — single showcase covering the full 12-mansion collection; price, size and bedrooms shown as ranges ("From AED 50M", "6–8 bedrooms", "20,383–39,004 sq ft BUA")
- Al Furjan — single community showcase covering studios, 1, 2 and 3-bedroom and terrace apartments; pricing on application
- Nine renders per mansion showcase: aerial, exterior, lagoon pool, rooftop pool, terrace, formal living, master bedroom, cinema, entertainment lounge
- Four renders per Al Furjan showcase: community view + three retail/street views
- Source files trimmed from almost a gigabyte of eight-thousand-pixel renders down to ten megabytes — sharp on screen, light on the site
- Detail pages show View, Plot, Size, Bedrooms and Payment Plan; per-unit numbers and prices are confirmed on enquiry, never displayed

## New Pages (Ready for Content)

- `/services` — sticky sub-nav and a calm section for each specialist offering
- `/team` — grid that fills automatically as advisors are added in the editor
- `/faq` — eight common questions in plain UK English, plus a short version on the homepage
- `/careers` — proper listings imported into the editor and a Life at Haus of Estate section ready for culture photos

## Careers Applications (Handled Properly)

- Upload a CV (PDF, DOC or DOCX)
- Choose years of experience and the kind of opportunity
- Tick a GDPR consent box
- Application lands at hr@hausofestate.com with the CV attached
- Automatic confirmation sent back to the applicant
